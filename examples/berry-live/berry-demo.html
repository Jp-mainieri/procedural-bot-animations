Berry — faithful engine port

DevBerry's actual mascot: the exact body + 3 leaves geometry, the exact spring/leaf-bend physics, and three of its real keyframed reactions, from lib/berry/{rig,motor,folhas,reacoes}.ts (branch feat/berry-simulador-mascote), translated to English identifiers but otherwise unchanged. Move your pointer over it; click or tap to jump, or use the buttons below.

This file is a reference demo, not part of the installable procedural-bot-animations package — Berry has a multi-part rig (leaves with their own spring physics), which is a different shape than the single-silhouette avatars that package's generic engine targets. See berry-case-study.md.
