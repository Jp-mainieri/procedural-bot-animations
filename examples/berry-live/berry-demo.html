DevBerry's actual mascot: exact body + 3-leaf geometry, exact spring/leaf-bend physics, real eye saccade/pursuit physics, weighted moods, loop activities, the hover/click gesture picker, and 20+ keyframed reactions — ported from lib/berry/{rig,motor,folhas,humores,olhar,foco,interacoes,reacoes,cerebro}.ts on branch feat/berry-simulador-mascote. Move your pointer over it, hover, click (click fast a few times), or try the gaze modes and focus test below.

reaction: none · gesture (hover/click): —

Mood (single, blended smoothly)

Each mood is a full-weight pose delta (eyelids, leaf lean, a touch of turn); the brain smooths the weight on every switch, so there's never a hard cut — see berry-moods.js.

Activity (looping, single)

Loop activities run as long as they're active, driven by the clock (berry-reactions.js's ACTIVITIES) rather than a fixed duration. Switching away from "sleeping" wakes Berry up first.

Reactions (one at a time; a new one fades the last out over 250ms)

Interactions (try them on the character itself)

Hovering picks a weighted gesture (never repeating the last). Hold the cursor over Berry for 2.5s for a cuddle, 9s to nap (click to wake). A single click also picks a gesture; quick clicks chain — the 3rd huffs, 5th gets dizzy, 8th hides.

"Reduced motion" stops idle sway, breathing, blinking and gaze wander — only discrete pose changes (mood, reaction) still show, per prefers-reduced-motion best practice.

This file and everything in examples/berry-live/ is a reference demo, not part of the installable procedural-bot-animations package — Berry has a multi-part rig (leaves with their own spring physics) and a much richer composition (moods, activities, gesture picker) than that package's generic single-silhouette engine targets. See berry-case-study.md for exactly what's ported and what isn't (Chama, the object-morph transformations).
